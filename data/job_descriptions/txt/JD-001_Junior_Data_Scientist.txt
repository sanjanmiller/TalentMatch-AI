Job Title
---------
Junior Data Scientist

Company Overview
----------------
Kaveri Insights Pvt. Ltd. is an analytics services company based in Bengaluru. It builds
reporting and predictive analytics solutions for retail, consumer goods and logistics
clients across India. Its data science team works in small project squads alongside
business analysts and data engineers.

Role Summary
------------
The Junior Data Scientist supports project squads by preparing data, exploring it,
building baseline predictive models and presenting findings to business stakeholders.
The role suits early-career candidates who can show hands-on practice through
internships, academic projects or personal projects, and who want to learn under the
guidance of senior data scientists.

Key Responsibilities
--------------------
- Extract and join data from relational databases using SQL, then clean, validate and
  transform it with Python (Pandas, NumPy).
- Carry out exploratory data analysis, document data-quality issues and summarise
  patterns using appropriate descriptive statistics.
- Apply hypothesis tests (for example t-tests and chi-square tests) to business
  questions and report results with clear interpretation.
- Build and evaluate baseline machine learning models (regression, classification,
  clustering) with Scikit-learn under senior review.
- Create charts and dashboards in Power BI, Tableau, or Matplotlib and Seaborn.
- Present findings to non-technical stakeholders through short written summaries and
  walkthroughs.
- Keep notebooks and code in Git with readable comments.

Required Technical Skills
-------------------------
- Hands-on proficiency: Python with Pandas and NumPy for data cleaning and manipulation.
- Hands-on proficiency: SQL, including joins, aggregations, filtering and basic window
  functions.
- Hands-on proficiency: exploratory data analysis and descriptive statistics.
- Working knowledge: statistics fundamentals, including distributions, confidence
  intervals and hypothesis testing.
- Working knowledge, shown in at least one project: Scikit-learn models such as linear
  and logistic regression, decision trees, random forests and k-means, with train/test
  splits, cross-validation and metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall and RMSE.
- Hands-on use of at least one visualisation tool: Power BI, Tableau, Matplotlib or
  Seaborn.
- Basic familiarity is sufficient for Git and Jupyter notebooks.

Required Experience
-------------------
- 0 to 2 years of relevant experience.
- Internships, academic projects, hackathons and personal projects count when they show
  end-to-end analysis work: data cleaning, analysis and a clear conclusion.
- Candidates with up to 2 years of professional experience should have used Python and
  SQL in real project work.
- Production deployment experience is not expected.

Education
---------
Bachelor's degree (B.Tech, B.E., B.Sc., BCA) or Master's degree (MCA, M.Sc.) in Computer
Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Data Science, Information Technology or a related
quantitative field. Candidates from other disciplines may be considered if they show
equivalent hands-on data skills through projects or coursework.

Certifications
--------------
Mandatory certifications: None.
Preferred certifications (not mandatory): Microsoft Power BI Data Analyst (PL-300),
Tableau Desktop Specialist, or a data analysis or machine learning course certificate
from a recognised online provider.

Preferred Qualifications
------------------------
- Experience with retail, logistics or e-commerce analytics data.
- Exposure to A/B testing concepts.
- Familiarity with Plotly or Streamlit for sharing results.
- Use of Excel for ad hoc analysis.
- Participation in data hackathons or open-dataset competitions.

Evaluation Priorities
---------------------
- Evidence of hands-on Python and SQL use in an internship, job or project, described
  through specific tasks rather than skill lists alone.
- Project evidence of data cleaning, exploratory analysis and at least one model
  evaluated with appropriate metrics.
- Use of statistical methods such as hypothesis testing, with interpretation of the
  result.
- At least one visualisation or dashboard deliverable.
- A relevant degree. Certifications are preferred only and are not required.

Employment Details
------------------
- Location: Bengaluru, Karnataka
- Employment type: Full-time, permanent
- Work arrangement: Hybrid, three days per week in office
