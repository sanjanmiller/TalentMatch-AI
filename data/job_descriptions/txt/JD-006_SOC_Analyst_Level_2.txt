Job Title
---------
SOC Analyst – Level 2

Company Overview
----------------
Sentrix Cyber Defence Pvt. Ltd. is a managed detection and response company based in
Gurugram. It operates a 24x7 security operations centre serving banking, telecom and
e-commerce clients in India.

Role Summary
------------
The Level 2 SOC Analyst investigates incidents escalated by Level 1, works independently
on deep-dive analysis across SIEM, EDR, network and identity data, coordinates
containment, and improves detections. The role also guides Level 1 analysts and writes
incident reports for client security teams.

Key Responsibilities
--------------------
- Investigate escalated alerts and perform deep-dive analysis across SIEM, EDR, network
  and identity logs.
- Develop and tune SIEM queries and correlation rules in KQL, SPL or AQL to improve
  detection and reduce false positives.
- Use EDR tools to investigate endpoints, contain hosts and collect triage artefacts
  under approved procedures.
- Carry out hypothesis-based threat hunts guided by MITRE ATT&CK.
- Coordinate incident containment and response with IT teams and application owners, and
  guide Level 1 analysts.
- Analyse Windows and Linux logs, including Sysmon, authentication logs and syslog, to
  establish scope and timeline.
- Perform root-cause analysis and write incident reports with clear findings and
  recommendations.
- Update runbooks and propose detection improvements.

Required Technical Skills
-------------------------
- SIEM query development and correlation in at least one of Microsoft Sentinel (KQL),
  Splunk (SPL) or IBM QRadar (AQL).
- EDR investigation with Microsoft Defender for Endpoint, CrowdStrike, SentinelOne or a
  comparable tool.
- Windows and Linux log analysis, including Sysmon.
- MITRE ATT&CK: mapping alerts and detections to techniques.
- Incident investigation and response: containment, eradication guidance, timeline
  reconstruction and root-cause analysis.
- Network analysis using DNS, proxy and firewall logs and packet captures.
- Use of threat intelligence for indicator enrichment.
- Incident reporting for technical and non-technical audiences.

Required Experience
-------------------
- 2 to 5 years in a SOC or security operations role.
- At least 1 year handling escalated incidents independently, at Level 2 or an
  equivalent level.
- At least one example of writing or tuning a SIEM detection rule or query.
- Participation in at least one incident that involved several teams.

Education
---------
B.Tech, B.E., BCA, B.Sc., MCA or M.Sc. in Computer Science, Information Technology,
Cybersecurity, Electronics or a related field. Equivalent experience with relevant
security training may be considered.

Certifications
--------------
Mandatory certifications: None.
Preferred certifications (not mandatory): Microsoft SC-200, Splunk Core Certified Power
User, CompTIA CySA+, GIAC Certified Incident Handler (GCIH), or Certified SOC Analyst
(CSA).

Preferred Qualifications
------------------------
- Threat hunting experience.
- SOAR playbook development.
- Scripting in Python or PowerShell.
- Cloud security logs from Azure or AWS.
- Vulnerability management exposure.
- Digital forensics tooling.

Evaluation Priorities
---------------------
- Investigation of escalated incidents with described scope, containment and outcome.
- SIEM query or rule development and tuning, shown with specific examples.
- EDR use in real investigations.
- Incident reports and root-cause analysis.
- Preferred capabilities such as threat hunting and SOAR are secondary to the required
  evidence. Certifications are preferred only.

Employment Details
------------------
- Location: Gurugram, Haryana
- Employment type: Full-time, permanent
- Work arrangement: Hybrid, three days per week in office, with rotational shifts and an
  on-call roster
