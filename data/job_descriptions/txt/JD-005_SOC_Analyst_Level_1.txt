Job Title
---------
SOC Analyst – Level 1

Company Overview
----------------
Cyberweave Security Operations Pvt. Ltd. is a managed security services provider based
in Navi Mumbai. Its 24x7 security operations centre monitors networks, endpoints and
cloud accounts for mid-sized enterprises in the finance, healthcare and manufacturing
sectors across India.

Role Summary
------------
The Level 1 SOC Analyst monitors security alerts, performs initial triage according to
documented runbooks, and escalates validated incidents to Level 2 analysts with clear
evidence. The role is designed for early-career professionals and does not require
incident-response leadership or independent threat hunting.

Key Responsibilities
--------------------
- Monitor SIEM alert queues during 24x7 rotational shifts and carry out initial triage
  using runbooks.
- Review logs from firewalls, endpoints, proxies, DNS and authentication systems to
  classify alerts as false positive, benign or suspicious.
- Investigate reported phishing emails by checking headers, URLs and attachments, and
  act according to procedure.
- Escalate validated incidents to Level 2 with a clear timeline and supporting evidence.
- Record incidents accurately in the ticketing system and maintain shift handover notes.
- Follow incident response procedures for common events such as malware, account
  compromise and phishing, under supervision.
- Identify noisy alerts and suggest candidates for false-positive tuning.

Required Technical Skills
-------------------------
- SIEM: working use of at least one of Microsoft Sentinel, Splunk, IBM QRadar, Elastic
  or a comparable platform for monitoring, searching and reviewing alerts. Lab or
  training experience is acceptable.
- Basic log analysis: Windows event logs, firewall, proxy and authentication logs.
- Networking fundamentals: TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP and HTTPS, common ports and basic packet
  inspection with Wireshark.
- Common attack indicators: phishing, brute-force attempts, malware beaconing and
  suspicious logins, with basic awareness of MITRE ATT&CK.
- Security fundamentals: confidentiality, integrity and availability, malware types,
  OWASP basics and identity and access concepts.
- Incident documentation and escalation practices.
- Basic familiarity is sufficient for EDR consoles and for scripting in Python,
  PowerShell or Bash.

Required Experience
-------------------
- 0 to 2 years of experience in security operations or a related role.
- Fresh graduates are acceptable with a SOC internship, home-lab or practical training
  that included SIEM use and alert triage.
- Willingness to work rotational shifts, including night shifts.
- Incident response leadership or threat hunting experience is not expected.

Education
---------
B.Tech, B.E., BCA, B.Sc. or MCA in Computer Science, Information Technology,
Cybersecurity, Electronics or a related field. A diploma with relevant security training
may be considered.

Certifications
--------------
Mandatory certifications: None.
Preferred certifications (not mandatory): CompTIA Security+, Microsoft SC-900, Microsoft
SC-200, Splunk Fundamentals or Core Certified User, or Certified SOC Analyst (CSA).

Preferred Qualifications
------------------------
- A home lab using tools such as Wazuh, Splunk or Elastic.
- Participation in capture-the-flag events.
- Use of a phishing or malware sandbox.
- Basic scripting for log processing.
- Exposure to ticketing tools such as ServiceNow or Jira.

Evaluation Priorities
---------------------
- Evidence of SIEM use, in a job, internship or lab, and of alert triage activity.
- Networking and log analysis fundamentals shown through projects, internships or
  coursework.
- Phishing analysis experience.
- Documentation and escalation practice.
- Absence of professional experience should not count against a candidate who shows
  practical evidence. Advanced incident response and threat hunting are not required.
  Certifications are preferred only.

Employment Details
------------------
- Location: Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra
- Employment type: Full-time, permanent
- Work arrangement: On-site at the security operations centre, 24x7 rotational shifts
  including nights
